# MSc Dissertation - Detecting AI-Generated fake reviews in e-commerce: An analysis of linguistic authenticity signals under the UK DMCCA 2024

This repository contains the analytical code and research outputs developed for an MSc Business Analytics dissertation at the University of Exeter Business School.

## Project summary

This study investigates whether linguistic authenticity signals remain reliable when fake reviews are generated by AI rather than written by humans.

Using two review datasets, the analysis compares the detection performance and diagnostic behaviour of linguistic signals across AI-generated and human-written fake reviews. Classical machine-learning models are combined with SHAP, LIME, and statistical analysis to examine both model-level performance and feature-level attribution.

The findings are interpreted through costly signalling theory and considered in relation to the responsibilities of e-commerce platforms under the UK Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (DMCCA).
